This is the first post of an engineering notebook. The idea is simple: have one place to
pin down the things I build and learn, instead of leaving them scattered across folders and notes.

## What you'll find here

I write about what I actually get my hands on:

- **Mechanics and design** - mechanisms, tolerances, parametric CAD, material choices.
- **Engines and drives** - actuators, drivers, a bit of control.
- **Wiring diagrams** - from concept to schematic to board.
- **3D prototypes** - printing, fast iterations, instructive mistakes.
- **Tools and data** - the small pieces of software that automate the boring work.

## How I see it

I care about the *why* behind the choices, not just the result. So the posts will try to
explain the constraints, the trade-offs accepted, and what I'd do differently - even when
something did **not** work (that's often where you learn the most).

Let's begin.
