Hi, I'm **Carlo**. This is my engineering notebook: a place where I collect notes,
experiments and projects spanning **mechanics, engines, design, wiring diagrams and
3D prototypes** - plus the software that holds it all together.

## What this blog is about

- **Mechanics & design** - mechanisms, tolerances, parametric CAD, DFM.
- **Engines & drives** - actuators, drivers, a bit of control.
- **Wiring diagrams & electronics** - from concept to schematic to board.
- **3D prototypes** - 3D printing, fast iterations, from idea to part.
- **Tools & data** - small pieces of software that automate design work.

## How I work

I like to start from the real problem, build the smallest version that works,
measure, and iterate - documenting the *why* along the way.
